# Content Strategy Memo: OTT Catalog Trends

**Audience:** Content acquisition and catalog strategy team  
**Purpose:** Turn catalog-level EDA into testable next steps  
**Data status:** Illustrative synthetic data; use as a demonstration only

## What the sample suggests
1. **The catalog is movie-led.** Movies account for approximately 67.7% of records in this sample. Consider whether the business objective calls for broad movie coverage, or whether serialized content could support repeat visits and longer engagement.
2. **Genre supply is uneven.** The largest primary genre in the sample is **Drama**. Compare genre supply with viewing hours, completion rate, subscriber cohorts, and acquisition cost before increasing investment; catalog count alone is not demand.
3. **Metadata completeness affects decision quality.** Country is unknown for 6.0% of cleaned records and director for 24.8%. Prioritize metadata enrichment because incomplete country/creator fields weaken regional and creator-level analysis.

## Three recommended actions
- **Greenlight a test, not a blanket expansion:** select 2–3 underrepresented genres and run a limited acquisition or promotion experiment. Measure starts, completion, repeat viewing, and incremental retention against a comparable control.
- **Build a regional portfolio view:** improve country metadata, then compare local-language availability with regional starts, completion, and subscriber growth. Do not use catalog representation as a proxy for local demand.
- **Create a content performance scorecard:** join catalog metadata to title-level impressions, starts, watch hours, completion, churn/retention, licensing cost, and release window. Rank candidates by incremental value, not title count alone.

## Guardrails
- These findings describe a synthetic demonstration dataset, not Netflix, Prime Video, or Hotstar.
- Release year is not the same as platform acquisition date.
- Ratings are content classifications, not audience scores.
- No causal claims can be made without performance data and a suitable experimental design.
